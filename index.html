Direct Ordering Guarantee & FAQ

Why order direct instead of Foodpanda?

Foodpanda extracts up to 30% commission, forcing higher prices. Ordering directly on KRAVE OS gives you 15% lower menu prices, dedicated kitchen riders, and 5% direct cashback on every single meal.

What if my meal is cold or delayed?

Tap our WhatsApp button or log a complaint above. Our branch manager resolves inquiries directly and re-delivers fresh food or issues instant wallet cashback within 5 minutes.

How does the DinePass Cashback Wallet work?

Every completed order credits 5% cashback straight into your DinePass wallet. It auto-deducts on your next checkout with zero expiry.

How does the 5-Stamp Free Burger work?

Each smash burger you order adds a stamp to your digital loyalty card. Once you complete 4 stamps, your 5th smash burger is 100% free!

Can I pay using State Bank of Pakistan's Raast QR?

Yes! Raast P2M QR is fully integrated with 0% fee. Pay instantly using Nayapay, SadaPay, HBL, Meezan Bank, or Easypaisa.

What is the minimum order value?

The minimum order subtotal is PKR 500. This ensures our dedicated riders can be fairly compensated with living wages.

How does Group Tray ordering work?

Click "Group Tray" on the menu tab and share your unique link. Friends can add their customized burgers from their own phones into one combined delivery.

Are deliveries halal and FBR POS compliant?

100% certified halal grass-fed beef and fresh buttermilk chicken. Every order is stamped with official Sindh Revenue Board / FBR QR fiscal invoice.
